# Nivara 3-Tier Safety & Escalation Engine

## Tiers
- **Green**: Standard supportive dialogues & grounding tools.
- **Yellow**: Moderate or compounding distress &rarr; discreet prompt to connect with college counsellor.
- **Red (Safety Mode)**: Crisis language detection &rarr; immediate modal with 1-tap call to Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1800-891-4416) and campus emergency contact.
